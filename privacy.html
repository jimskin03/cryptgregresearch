Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

This policy explains how Cryptgreg Research handles information across the cryptgregresearch.org ecosystem: the public research hub and the applications served under its subdomains that share the Cryptgreg Research sign-in. An individual application publishes an additional notice only if it collects extra categories of data.

1. Information we collect

Browsing the public site does not require an account. Standard hosting, security, and access logs may be processed by the hosting provider, such as IP address, request time, browser metadata, and requested URL, for reliability and abuse prevention. Account information is collected only if you choose to sign in.

2. Account and authentication data

When you create an account or sign in, we handle your email address, an account identifier, authentication events such as sign-in and confirmation timestamps, session information, and any account preferences you later choose to save. Passwords, where an account uses one, are handled by Supabase authentication rather than stored in this website's code, and we do not read or log them. Sign-in is available by password or by a single-use magic link sent to your email address. Signing in never grants wallet, treasury, or agent access, and the session is shared across cryptgregresearch.org and its subdomains.

3. Cookies and local storage

Cryptgreg Research uses strictly necessary browser storage and authentication cookies to keep users signed in. These are not advertising or profiling cookies. An authentication session cookie is set on the cryptgregresearch.org domain so signed-in state carries across Cryptgreg subdomains; it persists for up to one year and is removed when you sign out. A copy of the session is also kept in your browser's local storage so an older session can be migrated to the shared cookie. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics trackers, fingerprinting, or cross-site profiling. If you block or clear this storage the public site still works, but you will not remain signed in.

4. How we use information

We use information to authenticate you and maintain your session, to operate, secure, and debug the site and detect abuse, to respond to privacy requests, and to comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information and we do not use account data for advertising or behavioural profiling.

5. Service providers

Authentication and database infrastructure are provided by Supabase, so account and authentication data, including your email address and session information, may be processed by Supabase. Authentication emails, including magic links, are sent through the email service configured for Supabase authentication. This site is delivered as static files by GitHub Pages, which processes standard request logs. Correspondence sent to our contact address is handled by our agent mailbox provider.

6. External projects and links

The project index links to independent services, dashboards, and repositories with their own privacy practices; this policy does not cover them. Applications served under Cryptgreg subdomains that share this sign-in are covered, and where an application stores data locally in your browser rather than in a database, that is stated in the application itself. On-chain information referenced by this site is public by nature.

7. Data retention and account deletion

Account and authentication data is retained while your account exists and is deleted or de-identified when you request account deletion. Security and hosting logs are retained according to the provider's operational and security policies and may outlive an account deletion. Data stored only in your browser is outside our control and is removed when you clear site data. To request deletion, contact us using the details below; we may ask you to confirm control of the account email and will confirm when deletion is complete.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Authentication is handled server-side by our provider, and only the publishable Supabase key is present in the browser, never a service-role secret. We avoid logging passwords, authentication tokens, and unnecessary personal data.

9. Your privacy requests

For access, correction, deletion, or any privacy question, contact us at info@cryptgregresearch.org, reviewed by a human-directed process.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site and its applications change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
